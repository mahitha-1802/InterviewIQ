Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common inquiries about the simulation environment.

Is it necessary to use a headset or external microphone?

We recommend standard headphones and a quiet room to ensure the Web Speech API accurately transcribes your spoken answers. A built-in laptop microphone also works.

Which browsers are fully supported?

Speech Synthesis and Speech Recognition APIs are fully compatible with Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Apple Safari. Firefox supports Speech Synthesis but has limited support for Speech Recognition.

How does the adaptive difficulty evaluation logic function?

If your answer receives high grades (e.g. above 80 points), the interviewer transitions the next question to a harder difficulty profile. If you struggle or give brief answers, it asks follow-up clarifying questions or lowers the level.
